Indrashish Saha

PhD Candidate · Civil and Systems Engineering · Johns Hopkins University

I am a graduate student in the Department of Civil and Systems Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. My research focuses on data-driven computational mechanics, with a particular emphasis on high-strain-rate material behavior. I use crystal plasticity, damage mechanics, and computational modeling to study material deformation, fracture, and failure.

I completed my M.Tech. at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, and my undergraduate degree at Jadavpur University, Kolkata.

Research collaborations: I am open to collaborating on research projects. Please feel free to email me if you would like to discuss potential ideas.
